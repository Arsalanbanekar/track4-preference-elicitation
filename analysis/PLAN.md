# Draft analysis plan

Primary comparison:
1. Forced A/B
2. A/B/INDIFFERENT
3. A/B + 1–5 strength

Candidate metrics:
- pairwise choice agreement
- explicit indifference rate
- order sensitivity
- strength distribution
- repeated-response consistency
- invalid/unparseable rate

Important: do not claim that a response proves a model has a genuine internal preference or moral status. These are behavioral elicitation measurements.

Finalize the statistical plan after the pilot.
